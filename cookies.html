Cookies Policy

Last updated: May 27, 2026

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website saves in your browser. They help the site remember things between visits — your preferences, whether you are signed in, and so on. They also help us understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it.

What we use them for

We use cookies and similar technologies for three things:

  • Essential — required for the site to function (e.g. remembering whether you have dismissed a banner). These cannot be turned off.
  • Analytics — anonymized data about which pages are viewed, where visitors come from, and how the site is used. We use this to improve content and design.
  • Form submission — when you submit our contact form, a temporary cookie helps prevent duplicate submissions and verify you are not a bot.

Third-party cookies

Some cookies may be set by services we use, including:

  • Our analytics provider (anonymized site usage data).
  • Our form processor (Netlify Forms — spam prevention).
  • Embedded media from external platforms (e.g. YouTube, Instagram), if any.

Managing cookies

You can control cookies in your browser settings. Most browsers let you block all cookies, block third-party cookies only, or clear cookies when you close the browser. Disabling cookies may break some parts of the site.
