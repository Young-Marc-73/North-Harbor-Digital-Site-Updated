Built for performance

Modern Managed Hosting Infrastructure — our websites are deployed using a modern static hosting architecture designed for speed, reliability, security, and mobile performance. This approach reduces many of the common issues associated with traditional website hosting platforms, including plugin conflicts, slow load times, and unnecessary server overhead. We manage deployment, maintenance, uptime, and technical support so business owners can focus on running their business instead of managing technology.

Data Centers

Edge servers, close to your customers.

Your site is deployed to a global CDN with edge nodes across North America. Pages render in single-digit milliseconds whether the visitor is across town or three states away.

  • SSD-backed storage
  • Global edge CDN
  • HTTP/3 + Brotli
  • Sub-100ms TTFB
  • 99.99% uptime SLA
  • Auto-scaling traffic spikes
Network Specs

Bandwidth and latency that your customers actually feel.

10 Gbps uplinks at every datacenter, DDoS protection at the edge, and TLS 1.3 standard — your visitors don't see a slow site even on cellular.

  • 10 Gbps uplinks
  • DDoS mitigation
  • TLS 1.3 standard
  • IPv6 dual-stack
  • Anycast routing
  • Mobile-first delivery
Redundancy

If one node fails, your site doesn't.

Three independent regions, automatic failover, and rolling backups every 24 hours. Even when something goes wrong upstream, your visitors don't notice.

  • 3-region replication
  • Automatic failover
  • Daily off-site backups
  • Point-in-time restore
  • 24/7 uptime monitoring
  • Real-time status page

Critical patches in under 24 hours.

Backups

Daily snapshots, restored on request.

Every site is snapshot daily and held for 30 days. If something gets accidentally deleted — by you, by us, or by a typo in a CMS field — we can roll back to any prior day.

  • Daily off-site snapshots
  • 30-day retention
  • One-call restore
  • Point-in-time recovery
