Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 27, 2026

The short version

We are a small business that builds and hosts websites for service businesses. We collect only what we need to deliver our services and respond to inquiries. We do not sell your information, ever.

1. What we collect

When you fill out our contact form, we collect your name, email address, business type, and the message you send. When you use a site we host on your behalf, our hosting provider may log standard server data (IP address, user-agent, referer, request time) for security and reliability.

2. How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiry and provide a quote.
  • To deliver and maintain the services you have purchased.
  • To send occasional service updates (you can opt out any time).

3. Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers we use to operate the business — for example, our email provider, our hosting infrastructure, and our payment processor. Each of those vendors has its own privacy policy and processes data only on our behalf.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their marketing.

4. Cookies and analytics

We use a small number of cookies and analytics tools to understand how visitors use the site. See our Cookies Policy for details.

5. Your rights

You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email rightnow@rocketship.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Depending on where you live (e.g. California, EU, UK), you may have additional rights under laws like CCPA or GDPR. We honor those rights for everyone.

6. Data retention

We keep inquiry data for as long as needed to respond and follow up. Active client account data is retained for the life of the engagement plus seven years for tax and legal reasons. Server logs are typically retained for 30–90 days.

7. Security

We use SSL on every site we host, store secrets in a password manager, and follow industry-standard practices. No system is perfect, but we take this seriously.

8. Children

Our services are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Changes

We will update this policy when our practices change. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent revision.
